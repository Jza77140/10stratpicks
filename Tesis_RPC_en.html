1 Business model

Middle and lower-middle market private markets manager (the same registrant previously reported as P10, ticker PX). It structures and manages specialized funds and separate accounts across private equity, venture capital and private credit under brands such as RCP Advisors, Bonaccord, Qualitas, TrueBridge, Enhanced, Five Points, Hark and WTI. Revenue is almost entirely recurring management and advisory fees charged on committed capital with ten- to fifteen-year lock-ups. In 2025 it booked US$297.3M (US$296.4M in 2024) on FPAUM of US$29.4bn, which has compounded at 18% a year since 2020. It runs with 326 employees—137 investment professionals—across 12 offices in 9 U.S. states and one in Spain.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

Fees are charged on committed capital, not on market value: revenue is contracted for the life of the fund, typically ten to fifteen years, giving unusual visibility for an asset manager. The middle and lower-middle market is fragmented and hard to access, where relationships with fund managers matter more than size. No single client accounted for more than 10% of revenue in 2025, 2024 or 2023.
