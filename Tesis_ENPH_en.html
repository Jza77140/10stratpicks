1 Business model

Enphase designs and sells microinverters for residential solar systems: instead of one central inverter per home, it places one unit per panel, and on that foundation it has built batteries (IQ Battery), EV chargers and the Enlighten monitoring software. It makes money selling hardware to distributors and installers at manufacturer margins: in Q2-2026 it posted revenue of $291.9M with a non-GAAP gross margin of 46.8%, and shipped 1.59 million microinverters (725.2 MW DC) and 113.8 MWh of batteries. It manufactures in Texas and South Carolina, which turned out 1.58 million units in the quarter. The United States accounted for 83% of revenue in Q1-2026 and Europe grew 35% quarter over quarter in Q2. The business is cyclical and depends on how many rooftops get installed: Q2-2026 revenue fell 20% year over year and Q3 guidance ($290-320M) implies staying flat against a year that is already depressed.

Growth drivers: the first is the market's shift to the third-party ownership (TPO) model, where a financier rather than the household buys the system: so far in 2026 Enphase has signed roughly $1,080M in safe harbor agreements, of which it recognized $84.3M in Q2 and guides to ~$75M for Q3. The second is the new product cycle built on a GaN platform: the IQ9N residential microinverter (launched in June 2026, 97.44% EU weighted efficiency and a 25-year warranty), the 548 W IQ9S-3P commercial unit and the IQ Battery G5, which stacks 5 kWh modules up to 30 kWh with roughly 50% more energy density and about 40% lower cost. The third is the leap beyond the residential rooftop: the IQ Solid-State Transformer for AI data centers (1.25 MW rack, 98.5% efficiency), with a demonstration slated for late 2026 but no meaningful revenue until 2028-2029. The three levers are offsetting, not additive: they aim to plug the hole in residential demand, not to grow on top of it.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

Enphase is the clear leader of the microinverter niche -third-party estimates put its global share between 70% and 75%- and the US residential inverter market effectively operates as a duopoly with SolarEdge. The real advantage is not cost but channel and vertical integration: more than 25,000 installers certified to install its batteries, a proprietary ASIC (Kestrel) and a gallium nitride power platform that lets it refresh its product line without depending on a third party. That underpins pricing: even with revenue falling 20% year over year, the non-GAAP gross margin held at 46.8% in Q2-2026. The moat is moderate: it protects unit pricing, not volume, and it is worth nothing when the end market contracts by decree. On top of that, the migration to TPO moves the purchase decision from the household -which valued the brand and the 25-year warranty- to the financier, who buys on installed cost, eroding precisely the pillar the moat rests on.
A platform, not a component
Microinverter, battery, EV charger and Enlighten software are sold as an integrated system, which raises the cost of switching supplier midway through an installation.
Installer channel
More than 25,000 battery-certified installers as of Q2-2026, a channel built over years of training that cannot simply be bought.
In-house silicon
Kestrel ASIC and GaN power platform designed internally: it controls the cost curve and the pace of product refresh.
Verifiable pricing power
46.8% non-GAAP gross margin in a quarter with revenue down 20% year over year; pricing is not being defended with discounts.
US manufacturing
Texas and South Carolina shipped 1.58 million units in Q2-2026, which unlocks domestic content incentives and provides partial insulation from tariffs.

4 Risks● Key risk: High

End of the 25D credit and structural contractionThe 30% residential credit (Section 25D) expired on 31-Dec-2025 with no phase-down under the OBBBA: US revenue -83% of the total- fell 23% in Q1-2026 and the US residential market is projected to see installations down between 18% and 21% in 2026. This is not a cycle, it is the baseline shifting.
Safe harbor is a pull-forward, not new demandOf the $291.9M in Q2-2026, $84.3M was safe harbor revenue, and Q3 guidance includes roughly $75M more; this is equipment bought ahead of time to qualify for the ITC. Once that stock is worked through, the comparison runs against real demand.
The quarter's margin is inflatedThe 60.0% GAAP gross margin in Q2-2026 includes $45.4M of tariff refunds from US customs (+15.6 percentage points, a non-recurring event); at the same time reciprocal tariffs subtract about 2 points and Q3 guidance takes the non-GAAP margin down to 44-47%.
The valuation does not discount the declineAt 17.8x NTM earnings against a 5-year median of 20.4x it looks cheap, but consensus projects earnings growth of -14.19% per year over 5 years and an EBIT margin of 5.5% versus 14.7% over the last five years. On EV/FCF it trades at 30.5x against its own median of 27.3x: expensive on free cash flow of just $145.07M.
The data center pivot does not pay the bills todayThe IQ SST is a credible engineering bet, but the company's own timeline places the commercial impact in 2028-2029; it competes head-on with far larger power electronics manufacturers and has no connection to its solar installer channel.

5 Management

LeadershipBadri Kothandaraman has been CEO since September 2017 and Mandy Yang CFO since February 2022: the team that built the business is also the one that has to manage its contraction. In February 2026 it cut roughly 160 positions with a charge of about $4.6M, explicitly attributing the decision to the end of 25D.
CapitalIt closed Q2-2026 with $937.7M in cash and marketable securities against $572.8M of 2028 convertibles, and in March it retired the $632.5M of maturing convertibles with its own cash: net cash, ND/EBITDA of -1.40x. The share buyback has been suspended to preserve liquidity, a defensible decision but one that removes the prop under earnings per share.
PlanHold the margin with new product (IQ9N on GaN, IQ Battery G5 at roughly 40% lower cost), buy time with the safe harbor agreements with TPO providers and use the cash to fund the IQ SST through 2028. The plan is coherent; the fragile part is the bridge: between 2026 and 2028 the only verifiable source of incremental revenue is pull-forward purchases.
