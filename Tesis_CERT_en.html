1 Business model

Certara provides biosimulation software (mathematical modeling of how a drug behaves in the body) and regulatory-technical services that help pharma companies design clinical trials and build the filing package for the FDA and other agencies. By its own data, its customers received 90% or more of FDA new-drug approvals since 2014. Software is ~44% of revenue and services ~56%, with more than 160,000 licensed software users and 20 global regulatory agencies as direct software clients. EBIT margin is barely positive after a restructuring year, but FCF sits at 20% of sales.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

The biosimulation market is fragmented and competes with open-source software (R, PK-Sim) and pharma companies' in-house teams, but Certara is embedded in customers' validated workflows and in the documentation filed with regulators: switching vendors means revalidating an entire regulatory process. Its software is directly licensed by the FDA and 19 other agencies, giving it a de facto standard status in the drug-approval process.
