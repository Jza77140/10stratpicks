1 Business model

AppLovin sells performance advertising powered by its AXON AI engine: advertisers pay per install or sale and the algorithm decides in real time which ad to show across millions of apps. It has already divested its own games business: today it is 100% advertising software, with a record 85% adjusted EBITDA margin and very high cash conversion. Milestone of the year: in June 2026 it opened AXON to the public — after 14 years as a closed platform, any advertiser can now sign up and launch campaigns self-service.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

An oligopolistic mobile adtech market where it competes with Meta, Google and Unity. Its edge is the data flywheel: more advertisers → better model → better returns → more budget. That edge is real but algorithmic, not structural: it depends on keeping the model lead and on Apple/Google not changing targeting rules. Opening AXON widens the addressable market but also exposes the product to a far less sophisticated audience.
