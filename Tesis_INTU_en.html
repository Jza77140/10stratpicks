1 Business model

The dominant consumer and SMB finance platform in the U.S.: TurboTax (taxes), QuickBooks (SMB accounting and payroll), Credit Karma (personal finance) and Mailchimp (marketing). Subscription model with an ecosystem that cross-leverages data across products and migrates customers toward AI-assisted and live-expert services.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

Near-monopoly in U.S. DIY taxes and SMB accounting: high switching costs (historical data, accountant, integrations) and brand. The threat is not a direct competitor but IRS Direct File and generalist AI commoditizing simple returns.
