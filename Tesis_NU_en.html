1 Business model

Latin America's leading digital bank, with 139 million customers across Brazil, Mexico and Colombia. In Q2'26 it posted its first quarter with net income above $1,000M ($1,100M, +49% year over year) and a record 33% ROE, on a consolidated credit portfolio of $39,400M (+37% year over year). It trades at a 19.4x LTM P/E, with expected 3-year growth of +27%.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

Nu competes with traditional Latin American banking (Itaú, Bradesco, Banorte, Bancolombia) and other regional neobanks. Its edge is a cost to serve far lower than traditional banking and a customer base that already exceeds several incumbent banks in the region.
Cost to serve
A 100% digital structure with no branch network lets it operate at costs well below traditional banking
Regional scale
139 million customers, the largest digital-banking customer base in Latin America
Proprietary data underwriting
A proprietary model (NuFormer) that improves credit-origination quality across every income segment
Profitability
A record 33% ROE that funds growth without additional leverage
