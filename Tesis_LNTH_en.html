1 Business model

A radiopharmaceutical company with three product categories: Radiopharmaceutical Oncology (PYLARIFY, the PET agent for prostate cancer), Precision Diagnostics (DEFINITY, Neuraceq, TechneLite) and strategic partnerships. In 2025 revenue reached $1,541.6M (+0.5%): PYLARIFY $989.1M (-6.5%), DEFINITY $330.2M (+3.9%) and Neuraceq $51.4M. In July 2025 it acquired Life Molecular Imaging for $352.9M in cash, with up to $400.0M more in earn-outs, and on 1-Jan-2026 it sold the SPECT business to SHINE for up to $155.0M. It sells mainly to U.S. hospitals, independent imaging centers and government facilities (94.6% of revenue).

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

PET radiopharmaceuticals have half-lives measured in hours: they must be produced and distributed through a regional network of radiopharmacies, which is a genuine physical barrier. The offset is that regulatory exclusivity on the lead product expires soon and reimbursement changes can shift volume overnight.
Unique asset
A production and distribution network for short half-life PET agents that cannot be improvised
Regulatory barrier
Six PYLARIFY patents listed in the Orange Book, the last expiring in 2037
Brand
DEFINITY is an established standard in contrast echocardiography
