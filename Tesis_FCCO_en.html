1 Business model

South Carolina bank holding company and owner of First Community Bank (Lexington), with its main office plus 21 full-service offices spread across the Midlands (Lexington, Richland, Newberry and Kershaw counties), the Upstate (Greenville, Anderson and Pickens), the Piedmont (York) and the CSRA area (Aiken in South Carolina, and Richmond and Columbia in Georgia). At 31-Dec-2025 it held $2,058M in assets, $1,311M in loans, $1,750M in deposits and $167.6M of equity. In 2025 net interest income was $62.0M (+19.2%) and noninterest income $16.9M, equal to 21.5% of operating revenue, for net income of $19.2M, or $2.47 per diluted share. The tax-equivalent NIM rose 31 basis points to 3.23%, with a 0.94% ROA, 12.36% ROE and a 13.68% ROTCE. It reports four segments: commercial and retail banking, mortgage banking, investment advisory and non-deposit, and corporate.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

A $2,100M bank in South Carolina and Georgia markets where it competes against national and regional institutions with far more capital. Its real position rests on branch density in three counties — Lexington, Richland and Greenville — and on cheap funding: the average loan-to-deposit ratio is only 73.35% and it held no brokered deposits at year-end 2025. It is a well-funded bank with good fee income, not a bank with a barrier to entry.
