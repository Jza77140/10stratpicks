1 Business model

Idaho gold producer that also controls rare earth element properties listed in the U.S. national inventory. It mines the wholly owned Golden Chest mine in the Murray Gold Belt of the Coeur d'Alene mining district and processes the ore at the 360-tonne-per-day flotation plant of the New Jersey Mill, which it majority-owns and operates. It sells gold concentrate plus some doré. In 2025 it produced 12,538 ounces of gold, booked $42.4M of revenue (+64.6%) at a realized price of $3,583.43 per ounce versus $2,306.86 in 2024, and earned $16.6M at a 61.8% gross margin (50.3% in 2024). It employs 62 full-time people.

Growth drivers: the high-grade H-Vein at Golden Chest — 41,840 tonnes mined at 10.14 grams of gold per tonne in 2025 and the primary ore source expected for 2026; underground development, with 315 metres advanced on the MAR ramp; exploration across more than 20,000 consolidated acres of the Murray Gold Belt, including Niagara (copper-silver) and Little Baldy; and the rare earth properties Mineral Hill, Lemhi Pass — recognized by the USGS as the top thorium prospect in the U.S. — and Diamond Creek.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

This is a junior miner with a single producing asset and no control over the gold price. What sets it apart is an unusual combination: gold production generating cash today and a domestic rare earth position recognized by the USGS, the Idaho Geologic Survey and the Department of Energy, at a moment of political focus on critical minerals. That rare earth position generates no revenue yet.
Unique asset
The largest private land and mineral claim position in the Murray Gold Belt: over 20,000 acres consolidated for the first time in more than a century
