1 Business model

Externally managed BDC that lends directly originated senior secured debt to private equity-owned U.S. middle market companies. It is managed by Churchill DLC Advisor, with Churchill Asset Management and Nuveen Asset Management as sub-advisers; Nuveen is the asset management arm of TIAA. At 31-Dec-2025 the portfolio was $1,962M at fair value across 227 companies: 89.5% first-lien term loans, 8.2% subordinated debt and 2.3% equity investments. 94.1% of the debt pays a floating rate and the weighted average yield at cost was 9.48%. NAV per share closed at $17.72 (from $18.18) and net investment income was $1.86 per share.

Growth drivers: Churchill's proprietary deal flow — it oversees more than $63 billion of committed capital, deployed over $16 billion in 2025 and has cultivated relationships with more than 750 private equity firms; the recovery in private equity M&A activity, which gained traction in the fourth quarter of 2025; deliberate diversification, with an average position of 0.44% of the portfolio and the largest at 1.57%; and share repurchases below NAV, which took the count from 53.4 to 49.4 million shares.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

Private credit to the U.S. middle market: it lends to companies generating $10M to $100M of annual EBITDA, where banks have retreated and the private equity sponsor has both the incentive and the capacity to support the company. The real barrier is access to deal flow, and there Churchill competes on relationships and scale rather than price. The structural risk is that competition compresses spreads — and that is already happening.
Unique asset
Churchill's origination platform: more than 750 private equity firm relationships and over 200 dedicated professionals
Scale
More than $63 billion of committed capital under management allows it to enter large deals with low concentration
Regulatory barrier
BDC regime under the 1940 Act and an exemptive order allowing co-investment with Nuveen affiliates
No moat of its own
The moat belongs to the manager, not the vehicle: NCDL pays management and incentive fees for that access
