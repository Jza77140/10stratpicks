1 Business model

A three-sided platform: Mobility (rides), Delivery (food and retail) and Freight. It owns no vehicles: it matches supply with demand and keeps a commission (the take rate). Present in more than 70 countries.

The lever is margin, not volume. After years of subsidies the focus shifted to monetising the installed base: in-app advertising (high margin), the Uber One subscription, and running Mobility and Delivery over the same driver and the same user. Autonomy is the fork in the road: either the largest cost saving in its history, or the event that takes the network out of its hands.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

The network effect is local, not global: density is won city by city and does not transfer across markets. In each city, whoever has more drivers offers shorter waits, and shorter waits bring more riders.
