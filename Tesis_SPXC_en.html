1 Business model

Diversified industrial group with operations in 16 countries and sales in more than 100, organized into two segments. HVAC ($1,518.2M in 2025) makes cooling towers, process cooling and engineered air movement products for industrial markets — including data centers and power generation — plus hydronic and electric heating (Marley, Recold, TAMCO, Ingénia, Weil-McLain, Berko brands). Detection and Measurement ($746.9M) makes underground pipe and cable locators, inspection robotics, transportation systems and aids to navigation (Radiodetection, Cues, ULC Robotics, Genfare, Sealite). Revenue was $2,265.1M (+14.2%) in 2025 with $350.4M of operating income, and it closed with a combined backlog of $934.8M versus $657.7M a year earlier.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

These are engineered-equipment niches where the product is specified into the customer's project and then maintained for decades. Competition is on technical capability and service rather than list price, and installation and spare parts generate a post-sale stream. The cycle depends on capital expenditure and maintenance by industrial and institutional customers.
Brand
A portfolio of leading niche brands: Marley in cooling, Radiodetection in locating, Genfare in transit fare collection
Switching costs
Equipment is specified and installed into the customer's plant or system; replacing it means redesign and downtime
Scale
An M&A platform that buys niche leaders and gives them channel, engineering and financial backing
