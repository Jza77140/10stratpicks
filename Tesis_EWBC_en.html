1 Business model

Holding company of East West Bank, a California-based commercial bank specialized in the United States–Asia business corridor. It runs more than 110 locations: 96 U.S. branches (California, Texas, New York, Washington, Georgia, Massachusetts and Nevada), branches in China and Hong Kong, and representative offices in China and Singapore; it is the only U.S. regional bank with a commercial banking license in China, through its subsidiary East West Bank (China) Limited. It closed 2025 with $80.4 billion in assets, $56.9 billion in loans and $67.1 billion in deposits, all three growing 6%. Net interest income was $2,553 million (+12%) with a 3.41% NIM, noninterest income $379 million and total revenue $2,932 million. Net income reached $1,325 million (+14%), with a 1.70% ROA, 16.01% ROAE, 16.99% ROTCE and a 35.69% efficiency ratio.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

A large regional bank focused on the Asian-American community and businesses and on bilateral trade with Asia, a niche where it competes with national banks without meeting them head-on. Its Chinese commercial license is unique among U.S. regionals and lets it lend and take deposits on the mainland. International operations remain small, however: the Hong Kong branch contributed 3% of 2025 revenue and the China branches 1%.
