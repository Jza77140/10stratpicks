1 Business model

Community platform (subreddits) monetized mainly through contextual and interest-based advertising, plus a content licensing line used to train large language models. FY2025 revenue reached $2,202.5M (+69.4%): advertising $2,062.5M and other revenue —licensing and products— $140.0M. It averaged 121.4 million daily active uniques (DAUq) and 471.6 million weekly uniques in Q4-2025, across more than 100,000 active communities and an archive of over 2 billion posts and 22 billion comments. It crossed into profit: net income of $529.7M versus a $484.3M loss in 2024, with adjusted EBITDA of $845.1M and 2,555 employees. It went public in March 2024, so its track record as a listed company is very short.

Growth drivers: becoming a search destination —Reddit Answers is already integrated into core search and more than 80 million people searched on Reddit weekly in Q4-2025—; internationalization, with 68.9 million DAUq outside the U.S. (+28% year over year), machine translation live in 35 languages and roughly 1 billion posts translated; eight stated advertising monetization levers (auto-bidding, Max campaigns launched in January 2026, Dynamic Product Ads, new formats and new verticals such as automotive, retail and consumer packaged goods); and the expansion of data licensing agreements with technology and market-intelligence partners.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

It competes for screen time and for advertising budget against Google, Meta, TikTok, X, Snap and Pinterest, all of them better resourced. Its difference is structural: content is created and curated by anonymous users and volunteer moderators, organized by interest rather than by followers, which produces an inventory of recommendations with high purchase intent. Advertising rests on first-party data (communities visited, content engaged with) rather than third-party cookies, which makes it less exposed to privacy changes across the ecosystem.
Network effect
More than 100,000 active communities created and moderated by volunteers: more users generate more content, which attracts more users
Unique asset
A corpus of 2 billion posts and 22 billion comments accumulated since 2005, the basis of its licensing to AI models
Brand
Reddit is shorthand for human, non-advertorial recommendations; roughly 400 trademark registrations and applications worldwide
Proprietary data
Context and interest targeting built on first-party data, with no reliance on third parties
