1 Business model

Envela buys, authenticates and resells jewelry, gold, silver and luxury goods through its DGSE (Dallas Gold & Silver Exchange) brand, and offers commercial IT asset disposition (ITAD), de-manufacturing and recycling services to corporate clients, including Fortune 500 companies, through ECHG. The model is asset-light: it buys from individuals and institutions, appraises with in-house experts, and resells at a margin. A 15.6% ROIC and net debt/EBITDA of just 0.65x reflect a profitable, cash-generative operation.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

Jewelry and precious-metals re-commerce is a highly fragmented market, with pawn shops, local jewelers and online buyers competing without structural entry barriers. Envela's edge comes from execution and trust: in-house appraisal experts with decades of track record (DGSE has operated since the 1960s) and ITAD contracts with large corporations that value certified secure disposal, more than a hard economic moat.
