1 Business model

Bank holding company headquartered in Birmingham, Alabama, running only 33 full-service offices across Alabama, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee and Virginia, plus one loan production office in Florida. Its model is deliberately simple: organic loan and deposit growth driven by experienced bankers, on a centralized risk and credit platform with decentralized decision-making in the hands of regional CEOs. It closed 2025 with $17.73 billion in assets, $13.70 billion in loans and $14.22 billion in deposits. Net interest income rose to $535 million with a 3.12% NIM —up from 2.82% in 2024— and noninterest income was only $27 million. Net income reached $277 million (+22%), with a 1.56% ROA and a 16.05% ROE, while noninterest expense stayed at $185 million.

3 Competitive advantage (MOAT)

A Southeastern commercial bank that competes on relationships and speed of decision rather than on network. Its five largest competitors are Regions Financial, Wells Fargo, PNC, Truist and Pinnacle Financial Partners, all with greater resources, higher lending limits and more advertising. The flip side of the branch-light model is a structurally low operating cost, which lets it pay more for deposits and charge less for loans without losing profitability.
Structural efficiency
